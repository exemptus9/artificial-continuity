How do I catch a line after I’ve already said it?

For a fresh idea, tap Record my words in Android’s expanded overlay. Speak when the timer starts and tap Finish & edit. Listen to the original, correct the wording, then copy the passage or share the words or audio. A take saves after one minute even if you never tap Finish. For a look back, start listening before you begin. On Android, recent audio sits in a rolling 90-second buffer. Say “Lexi, keep the last minute” to save what is still there. For a longer draft, say “Lexi, start a passage,” speak through the pauses, then say “finish passage.” The voice-command guide lists the available phrases. For longer work, open Long sessions and record until Finish & save session. Mark a point without breaking the flow, rename the recording afterward, and export the AAC audio and marker index for the web player. Lock screen & sound can speak acknowledgments for creative saves, word candidates, markers and completed sessions. Two rising notes acknowledge the wake word.

What gets saved during an ordinary day?

Choose among three records. Selected clips keep moments you mark or the detector flags. The optional text journal keeps timestamped recognized speech throughout the session. Optional full audio keeps longer stretches of sound, including speech the recognizer misses. Review a candidate as worth keeping or less like it to adjust future suggestions. A new word, a revised line or a rhyme can all be reasons to flag a passage.

How does the phone learn my voice and my words?

While listening, open Live now, then Remember voices. Teach my voice takes a short phrase from one speaker. Check who spoke, edit what you actually said if needed, and save the sample. Try a name or invented word that the phone often misses. A future finalized line matched to your profile can use that wording. You can inspect and forget individual corrections, add samples, or remove a profile. You can also label another speaker or a media source. Short or overlapping voices can remain uncertain.

What helps with a quiet voice or a noisy room?

Android transcribes English on the device. In Capture choices, boost quiet speech or choose Learn room noise and give the phone two seconds of just the room. This reduces steady background sound in the live transcription input. Learn again after changing microphones or resuming a session. Saved clips retain the original audio. For a difficult saved passage, Analyze original recording can compare additional readings before adopting a change. Listen back to check a name, pun or line break.

What can I see while another app is open?

Drag the Android overlay from any point. The smallest strip shows elapsed time and microphone activity even before the first word registers. Hold the tiny readout to save the last minute. Between phrases it can show the available audio buffer or your saved and flagged totals. The × in every size asks before stopping recording and closing the app. Choose Go back to keep listening. Use its size control to move between a tiny status strip, a medium panel and a larger view with actions. Follow live words, input level, saves and flags. The expanded view shows the current and last distinct situation cue. An idea followed by a revision can suggest spoken drafting. Open Live & saved to examine the source words. You can pause, resume, or confirm that you want to end listening. Expand the lock-screen notification for input, word, save and flag counts. During listening, save the preceding minute, start or finish a voice take, or pause. After pausing, resume or choose End & close for a confirmation. You can swap Start take for Mark missed to save up to 90 seconds for review. Lock-screen word and situation previews are optional. Under Lock screen & sound, test the rising wake and falling save chimes, change their level, or enable a spoken confirmation after a requested save. Android controls which actions are available while locked.

How do I find a particular moment later?

Open Live & saved for recent words, journals, clips and recordings. Tap an individual passage in Full journal to edit, copy or share it. A journal entry preserves the original recognition alongside corrections when they differ. In the private archive, search for a phrase, date or category. From a timestamped journal line, Find audio here opens a recording covering that time if one exists. Play or export a clip, make an excerpt, file it in a collection or copy a draft into your writing app.

How do the phone, laptop and private archive work together?

Total Recall Desk can record from the laptop microphone and keep a recent moment independently while the laptop is awake. Pair the phone with Mint or your private hosted archive. Select clips, full audio and closed journals separately. Choose automatic batches, Wi-Fi and charging preferences. Interrupted transfers can resume and completed receipts verify file size and checksum. Overlapping device recordings stay available as separate originals.

What happens if the microphone or storage fills up?

If another app takes the phone microphone, Recall shows a waiting state, records the coverage gap, and retries when input returns. The laptop can provide independent coverage. For storage, choose a phone budget and free-space floor, skip near-silent full-audio blocks, and optionally reclaim old phone copies after a verified transfer. Archive & sync shows pending uploads, capacity issues and gaps so you can intervene.

Who can see my recordings?

The recorder keeps voice profiles on the phone. You choose whether to save a journal, retain full audio, show words on the lock screen, or transfer material to a paired archive. Private playback and archive controls require access to that device or archive. The early-access form below accepts invitation requests.
